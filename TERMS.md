# Terms of Use

_Last updated: 2026-10-04_

These terms cover **Tools for Abdul's deployment** of slack-solidarity-helper-tools at
`https://slack.tools4abdul.com`. By signing in, with Slack or with Google, you agree to them.
They sit alongside the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md), which says what is recorded about you and
for how long, and the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md), which says how to report a problem.

The short version: this is a volunteer-run tool for organizing a political campaign. Use it to
canvass for the campaign. Keep voter information to yourself. Don't try to get around the
limits. Organizers can turn off your access.

## Who runs this

The **Tools for Abdul** volunteer team runs this deployment. It is a volunteer project, not a
company, and it is offered free of charge. The source code is public at
[tools4abdul/slack-solidarity-helper-tools](https://github.com/tools4abdul/slack-solidarity-helper-tools).

## Who can use it

- **Members of the campaign's Slack workspace** sign in with Slack. What they can reach depends
  on the role organizers have given them.
- **Anyone with a Google account** can sign in with Google to use turf checkout: the canvassing
  map at `/turfs`, where you claim turf, walk it and mark it done. A Google sign-in reaches turf
  checkout and nothing else.

The app is not directed at children under 13. Canvassing may carry its own age requirements
under the campaign's volunteer guidelines; follow them.

## Using turf checkout

- **Claim turf you mean to walk**, and release it if your plans change, so someone else can take
  it. Claims lapse on their own after the time shown on the page.
- **Mark turf walked honestly.** The percentage you report is what organizers plan around.
- **Your MiniVAN list number is yours.** It loads the doors you claimed in MiniVAN. Don't share
  it, post it, or hand it to someone who has not claimed that turf.
- **Follow the campaign's canvassing guidance** and the law wherever you knock, including local
  rules on soliciting and private property.

## Voter information

Walking turf puts voter information in front of you, mostly in MiniVAN: names, addresses and
the like. That information belongs to the campaign and is held under the campaign's agreements
with its data providers. The law also limits how voter records may be used.

- Use it **only to canvass for this campaign**.
- **Don't copy, export, photograph, share or sell it**, or use it for any other purpose,
  personal or political.
- If you see something that looks wrong or sensitive, tell an organizer rather than acting on it
  yourself.

The turf map and the canvassing numbers in this app are campaign planning information too.
Don't republish them.

## What you must not do

- Use scripts, scrapers or other automation to read the map or claim turf.
- Use more than one account, or a new account, to get around a limit or a block.
- Sign in as, or pretend to be, someone else.
- Try to reach pages, data or other people's turf you have not been given, or interfere with the
  app or the services it connects to.
- Look for security weaknesses, except as the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) describes, and report
  what you find there.
- Put abusive, misleading or unlawful content anywhere the app shows it to others, including your
  display name.

## Organizers' decisions

Organizers may release turf you hold, block you from turf checkout, or remove your access at any
time, including without giving a reason, to keep the canvass running fairly and voter
information safe. A block takes effect immediately. If you think one is a mistake, ask an
organizer.

## Your account

apply to that account, and you are responsible for keeping it secure. The app asks Google only
for your name, email address and account ID, and asks Slack only who you are. See the
[Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) for what is kept and for how long. You can stop using the app at any
time by signing out.

## Other services

solidarity.tech and Mobilize. Each has its own terms, and nothing here changes them.
