WHY THESE ARE HERE
The Abdul for US brand guide specifies Kensington (Fort Foundry) for display and
Halyard Text (Darden Studio) for body copy. Both are distributed through Adobe
Fonts, which does not permit self-hosting — using them on the web requires a
Typekit web project tied to an Adobe subscription, with the site's domain
allowlisted.

Until that exists these two OFL faces stand in: Oswald for Kensington's narrow
all-caps headline voice, Source Sans 3 for Halyard's humanist body. They
approximate; they do not match.

To switch to the real faces: add the Typekit stylesheet to src/app.html and put
'kensington' / 'halyard-text' at the front of FONT_STACKS in
src/lib/styles/brand.ts. Nothing else changes.

Latin subset only, matching the Silkscreen vendoring alongside.
